# LinkedIn Posting Guide for ONTAP

## Post Structure

### Hook (First 2 lines - visible before "see more")
```
Just built ONTAP — a campus super app

Here's what I learned shipping a full-stack product...
```

---

## Key Elements to Include

1. **Problem Statement** - Why students need this
2. **Tech Stack** - React, Node.js, MongoDB, Express
3. **Features Highlights** - Pick 3-4 best: Marketplace, Rentals, MindWave AI, Rides
4. **Learnings** - What challenges you overcame
5. **Call to Action** - Ask for feedback or connections

---

## Video Tips

- Keep it **60-90 seconds** max
- Add **captions** (most watch on mute)
- Show the **mobile view** - looks more impressive
- Highlight **smooth interactions** and real data

## Post-Publishing Actions

1. **First Hour** - Reply to every comment
2. **Share** to relevant LinkedIn groups
3. **Cross-post** to Twitter/X with shorter version
4. **Pin** to your profile if it performs well
