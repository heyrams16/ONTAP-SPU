Just built ONTAP — a super app that brings the entire campus experience into one platform

As a student, I noticed we're constantly switching between apps to buy textbooks, find rides, rent equipment, and get study help.

So I built the solution.

**What ONTAP does:**
- Student marketplace for buying/selling
- Rentals for textbooks, electronics, furniture
- Rideshare to airports & nearby cities
- MindWave AI — study assistant, wellness coach, smart campus guide
- Mobile-first design inspired by Careem

**Tech stack:**
React · Node.js · Express · MongoDB · Axios

**What I learned:**
→ Building for real users changes everything
→ Proxy configuration headaches are real
→ Good UI/UX makes or breaks adoption

This started as a side project but turned into something I'm genuinely proud of.
